# 03C post-pass process review

Theme Park first regenerated and passed its own 03C products from Starter v10 and the repaired 02C semantic hash. Only afterward were Starter's 03C/nonrepetition rules and Rock n Roll Racing's matching final report/generation summary reviewed for method checks.

Retained process checks:

1. Keep lexical candidates, exact starts and production admissions as three disjoint authority levels.
2. Preserve vector-origin records even where target addresses coincide; deduplication belongs to the next exact-context join, not the evidence input.
3. Inventory APUIO/MMIO/WRAM interests early but keep DBR-dependent and all-offset matches non-authoritative.
4. Cold-regenerate products and bind reuse to ROM, mapper/profile, decoder semantic and candidate-policy hashes rather than a version number.
5. Carry explicit boundaries into 04C instead of treating the all-offset scan as a permanent instruction queue.

Rejected inputs: Rock's ROM identity, row digest, candidate/context counts, byte ownership, graph/frontiers, Mesen comparison, acceptance label and generated implementation. Theme Park uses none of them as authority. Under the current Starter v10 roadmap, exact direct-flow contexts are owned by 04C, not imported from Rock's historical 03C arrangement.
