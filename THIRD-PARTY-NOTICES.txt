Third-party notices

SDL 3.4.10
----------
SDL3 is statically linked for gamepad support. Its zlib license is included
as SDL-LICENSE.txt. The launcher uses SDL3's built-in controller mappings.
No separate controller-mapping database is distributed.

Original game
-------------
Theme Park, the original ROM and game assets are not distributed by this
project. Supply your own legally obtained supported ROM.

Development references
----------------------
Other SNES static recompilations and emulator reference material were used
for workflow and comparison. No external emulator is shipped, invoked or
used as a runtime fallback by this release.
